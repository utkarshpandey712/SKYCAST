    About SkyCast

    SkyCast is a free weather website that shows live conditions and forecasts for any city in the world. Search a city to see the current temperature, what it feels like, humidity, wind speed, UV index, sunrise and sunset times, an hourly forecast for the next 24 hours and a 5-day outlook. Save your favourite cities for one-tap access, switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit, and use dark or light mode.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where does SkyCast get its weather data?

    Weather and location data come from Open-Meteo, an open weather data service that combines national weather models.

    How often is the weather updated?

    Forecasts are refreshed from the source every few minutes whenever you search or reload a city.

    How do I switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit?

    Use the °C / °F switch at the top right. Your choice is remembered on your device.

    Can I see the weather for my current location?

    Yes. Tap “My location” and allow location access in your browser. Your location is only used to fetch the weather.
